DashcamViewer is an open-source Android app for the WANLIPO A13 dual-channel dash camera.

Browse recordings on the camera, play supported files, download videos and photos to your phone, view live video, and inspect camera status and settings.

Features:
• Connect to the A13 camera's Wi-Fi or use a Wi-Fi network you selected.
• Browse loop, event, emergency, parking, and race recordings.
• Play and download listed recordings; downloaded files remain available after disconnecting.
• View the camera's live H.264 stream when supported.
• Read camera status and advertised settings. Writes use explicit confirmation and readback where supported.
• Optionally remember the camera Wi-Fi password using encrypted local storage.

This app supports the investigated WANLIPO A13 profile only. Some camera features are not exposed through its interface; parking control may need the camera's physical menu. Rear-feed and live-view behavior depend on the camera hardware and have not been validated on all devices.

The app does not use accounts, analytics, cloud storage, or firmware updates. Camera access is local over Wi-Fi. Camera timestamps and reported file sizes may be inaccurate.
